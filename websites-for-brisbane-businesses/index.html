FAQ

Who is this website service actually for?

It is built for established businesses that need a stronger website, and for serious new businesses that are ready to do it properly from the start. If the business has a real offer, clear intent, and wants a custom site that supports growth, it is usually a fit.

What do you mean by no-BS web design?

It means no bloated process, no layers of account managers, and no vague upsell-heavy handover. The project stays focused on what the business actually needs: clear messaging, strong structure, proper design decisions, and a website that is easier to trust and easier to choose.

Why go custom instead of using a template?

A custom website gives more control over structure, messaging, SEO foundations, and how the site supports the way the business actually sells. Templates can work for simple placeholder sites, but they usually force the business to fit the template instead of the website fitting the business.

Can this work for a serious new business?

Yes, if the business is serious about getting the positioning, offer, and digital foundation right early. The point is not whether the business is brand new or established. The point is whether it is real, ready to invest properly, and wants a website built around where it is going.

How do you make sure the website is built around the business?

The structure, pages, messaging, and calls to action are shaped around the actual offer, customer, and sales process, not around a generic package. That usually means understanding what the business sells, what trust signals matter, what content is needed, and what the site needs to help people do next.

Do you still build for SEO and long-term usability?

Yes. The builds are set up so the site is easier to edit, expand, and support over time, with SEO foundations considered from the start. The goal is not just a site that looks good on launch day. It is a site that can keep working as the business grows.
