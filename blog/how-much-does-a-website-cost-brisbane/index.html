The Real Price Range In Brisbane

The difference comes down to how the site is built, how many pages you need, and whether the focus is purely design or performance and SEO. If you want a build that’s structured to convert and scale, start with our Website Design service and you’ll get a clear baseline for what’s involved.

What Impacts Website Cost?

Custom Build vs Template

Template sites are cheaper upfront but often come with performance limitations and messy structure. Custom-coded builds cost more initially, but they’re faster, cleaner, and built around long-term ranking and conversion. If you’re comparing builders based on visibility and leads, your SEO foundation matters just as much as the visuals.

Page Count

A 3-page brochure site costs significantly less than a 7–10 page conversion-focused build with dedicated service pages. If your goal is to rank for multiple services in Brisbane, you need pages that target those searches properly. That’s exactly why we build structured service pages into our web design builds.

SEO Foundation

A website built properly includes semantic headings, internal linking, metadata, schema, and performance optimisation. Many cheaper builds skip this entirely, which is why businesses end up paying again later. If you want the full picture of what “SEO-ready” actually means, see our SEO services.

Why Cheap Websites Usually Cost More Later

The $800–$1,200 range often means heavy themes, slow load times, and minimal SEO structure. That usually leads to poor rankings, low enquiries, and eventually a rebuild. If you’re unsure whether your current site is costing you leads, start with a proper audit and reach out via our contact page.

What A Performance-Focused Website Includes

A properly built site includes clean architecture, fast load times, mobile optimisation, clear service pages, and internal linking designed to support local visibility in Brisbane. This is the difference between a site that “exists” and a site that generates enquiries. If you want to see what that looks like in practice, our Website Design service outlines what’s included.

And once your site is live, keeping it fast, stable, and updated is what protects your rankings and performance long-term. That’s where ongoing Website Maintenance makes the difference.

How To Choose The Right Website Package

Don’t choose based on price alone. Choose based on outcome. If you need a simple presence online, you can keep it lean. If you need ranking, leads, and authority in Brisbane, you’ll want strong structure and an SEO foundation from day one. Final Thoughts

The real question isn’t “How much does a website cost?” It’s “What is the website built to do?” If it’s built to generate enquiries and rank locally, it’s an asset. If it’s built to look good and sit online, it’s an expense. If you want a site that performs, explore our web design services, or pair it with ongoing visibility through our SEO services.

FAQ

How much does a custom website cost?

Most small businesses spend anywhere from $1000 to $3,000+ depending on how the site is made, page count, design depth, copywriting, integrations, and how conversion-focused the build is. At The MX Studio, we price simply: $1500 for a 3-page build, then an additional $250 per page. Price per page can range from $150 to $350 depending on complexity and length. A standard site may need 7 pages, so you're looking at a minimum of $2500. If you want copywriting, multiple landing pages, and highly customised pages, that price point goes up.

Do I need a new website, or just an SEO and Google Ads refresh?

Not always. If your current site is structurally sound, sometimes the highest-impact move is fixing technical issues through SEO and layering in Google Ads rather than rebuilding from scratch. We'll tell you honestly on a discovery call if a redesign isn't actually what you need yet.

What information do you need from me to get started?

For a website build: your existing branding, or interest in brand design if you don't have one yet, a few reference sites you like, your service list, and any existing copy or photos. For SEO or Ads: access to your Google Business Profile and analytics, and clarity on which services matter most to your business.

Will I be able to update my own website after it's built?

Yes. Every site is built so you can make basic content edits yourself — text, images, blog posts — without calling us for small changes. Anything more involved, like a web redesign down the track, we handle directly.

How do you measure whether SEO or Google Ads is actually working?

Rankings and impressions are easy to point to, but they don't pay your bills — calls, form submissions, and bookings do. Maxx oversees reporting personally across every active SEO and Google Ads campaign, tracking real enquiry activity rather than vanity metrics like traffic or position changes.

How Is The MX Studio Different from a Typical Agency?

You're working directly with Maxx, not a rotating cast of account managers passing your project between departments. Maxx oversees every project personally from strategy through to launch — making the calls on design direction, SEO priorities, and what actually gets built — so nothing gets lost in hand-offs and you always know who's accountable for the result.

What Happens After My Website Goes Live?

Launch isn't the finish line. Every site comes with a check-in period to catch early issues, then moves into website hosting and maintenance if you want ongoing support, or SEO and Google Ads if you're ready to start driving traffic to it.

How Long Does a Typical Project Take?

Most websites go from kick-off to live in 4–6 weeks, depending on scope and how quickly content and feedback move back and forth. SEO and Google Ads work differently — SEO builds momentum over months, while Google Ads can start generating leads almost immediately once campaigns go live.
