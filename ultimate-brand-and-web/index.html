Hosting and support is a flat $100 monthly fee. Your website sits on our servers, with security, updates, backups, and the technical handling covered for you. You are getting a custom 20-page WordPress website, SEO foundations considered throughout the build, a professional brand identity, logo variations, business card designs, and reusable social assets like cover images, Instagram story templates, and carousel templates.
