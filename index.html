FAQ

How are website projects scoped?

Website projects are scoped around page count, copy, integrations, growth goals, and how custom the build needs to be. Some businesses need a leaner foundation, others need broader structure and more content depth. The useful number comes from the work the site actually needs to do, not from forcing every project into the same shape.

Do I need a new website, or just an SEO and Google Ads refresh?

Not always. If your current site is structurally sound, sometimes the highest-impact move is fixing technical issues through SEO and layering in Google Ads rather than rebuilding from scratch. We'll tell you honestly on a discovery call if a redesign isn't actually what you need yet.

What information do you need from me to get started?

For a website build: your existing branding, or interest in brand design if you don't have one yet, a few reference sites you like, your service list, and any existing copy or photos. For SEO or Ads: access to your Google Business Profile and analytics, and clarity on which services matter most to your business.

Will I be able to update my own website after it's built?

Yes. Every site is built so you can make basic content edits yourself — text, images, blog posts — without calling us for small changes. Anything more involved, like a web redesign down the track, we handle directly.

How do you measure whether SEO or Google Ads is actually working?

Rankings and impressions are easy to point to, but they don't pay your bills — calls, form submissions, and bookings do. Maxx oversees reporting personally across every active SEO and Google Ads campaign, tracking real enquiry activity rather than vanity metrics like traffic or position changes.

How Is The MX Studio Different from a Typical Agency?

You're working directly with Maxx, not a rotating cast of account managers passing your project between departments. Maxx oversees every project personally from strategy through to launch — making the calls on design direction, SEO priorities, and what actually gets built — so nothing gets lost in hand-offs and you always know who's accountable for the result.

What Happens After My Website Goes Live?

Launch isn't the finish line. Every site comes with a check-in period to catch early issues, then moves into website hosting and maintenance if you want ongoing support, or SEO and Google Ads if you're ready to start driving traffic to it.

How Long Does a Typical Project Take?

Most websites go from kick-off to live in 4–6 weeks, depending on scope and how quickly content and feedback move back and forth. SEO and Google Ads work differently — SEO builds momentum over months, while Google Ads can start generating leads almost immediately once campaigns go live.
