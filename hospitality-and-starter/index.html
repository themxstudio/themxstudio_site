FAQ

How is my website hosted and kept safe long term?

Hosting and support with us is required for this offer, and it is a flat $50 per month. We offer it this way to make everything easier for you. GoDaddy, the domain setup, and the technical handling are all covered, so you do not need to stress about it. It gives the site a safe place to live, handles automated updates, and if something breaks, we fix it. We will even walk you through getting your professional email up and ready. There are no minimum terms.

Can I still edit the site myself?

Yes. All clients receive a login, and the words and images are easy to edit yourself. If your site needs an editable menu, that can be managed there too.

Will there be hidden fees?

Likely no. Forms, email capture, editable menus, and map integration are all included. If you need something more complex, like a booking system, we'll provide a proper quote first.

Can I expand the site later if needed?

Yes. We'll make you a custom theme, which means when you're ready to spend more in the future, we already know how your site is built. We can expand the marketing, grow the site out later, and usually do it for less than a full rebuild would cost.
