FAQ

How much budget do I need for Google Ads?

It depends on the industry, competition, and how much demand exists around your services. The useful question is not the minimum spend, but whether the budget is enough to collect real data and compete for the searches that matter. A budget that never gathers meaningful conversion data usually makes decision-making slower, not safer.

Do I need a dedicated landing page before running ads?

Not always, but the page needs to match the search and make the next step obvious. If the current site is weak, slow, or vague, better landing pages can do more for results than endlessly tweaking the ads themselves. The closer the page feels to the intent behind the click, the easier it is to convert that traffic efficiently.

How quickly can Google Ads start generating leads?

Google Ads can move much faster than SEO because visibility starts once the campaign is live. The quality of the leads still depends on the offer, the landing page, the tracking, and whether the account is built around commercial intent rather than just traffic. Fast visibility is useful, but it still needs a trustworthy page and a clear next step to become real business.

Do you set up conversion tracking and reporting?

Yes. Ads should be measured against calls, forms, bookings, or actual enquiries, not just clicks. Tracking and reporting need to show whether the spend is producing useful business outcomes. That is what allows the account to improve based on real lead quality instead of surface activity.

Is Google Ads better than SEO for my business?

They solve different problems. Google Ads is faster when you need immediate search visibility, while SEO compounds over time and reduces how dependent you are on paid traffic alone. A lot of businesses need both, just in different proportions. Paid search captures demand quickly, while SEO builds a stronger long-term base around the same services.

Who owns the Google Ads account and data?

You should. The business should retain ownership of the account, conversion data, and tracking setup even when someone else is managing the campaigns. That keeps the history and control where it belongs. Long-term performance data is too valuable to lose inside someone else's account structure.
